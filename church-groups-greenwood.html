Church Groups – Greenwood

Stay and Play @Greenwood

Thursdays 10am–12noon (term time)

The group is run by members of the church who all have a background in Early Years Education and childcare. Most of the morning is devoted to free play, outdoor when possible. A children's snack of toast and fruit and a hot drink for adults is offered from 10.30am. Tidy up starts at around 11.30am and a rhyme/story time is offered at 11.45am.

We aim to provide:

  • A safe and caring environment which nurtures friendships
  • Activities and resources that encourage the physical, emotional, intellectual, and spiritual development of young children and their carers
  • Ideas and inspiration for activities to undertake at home to continue learning

We ask for a minimum donation of £1 per person (adult or child).

Greenwood Ramblers

Greenwood Ramblers meet monthly on a Saturday morning. We have our planning meeting in January and any members can lead a walk. Our group is not all church members as over the years friends of members have joined us. We are a happy, friendly group. We are a group of 12 in total but numbers on the walks vary. We have slowed down compared to the pace we used to set; we go at the pace of the slowest.

We usually meet at 10am and car share to the meeting point which is not more than 30–40 minutes' drive away. Our walks are approximately 4 miles. We finish with a pub/café lunch where possible.

Memory Café

First Thursday of the month, 1.30–3.30pm

The memory café is hosted by church members and facilitated by volunteers who have had professional experience of supporting those living with dementia. We provide a safe and comfortable space in which to chat, play games and enjoy a cuppa. For more information, please email stalbansurcgreenwood@gmail.com

Progressive Christian Network

Part of the wider Progressive Christian Network Britain, we meet in person at Greenwood on the second Thursday of the month, and by Zoom on the fourth Thursday of the month to listen to a speaker and discuss the topic. For more details, please see pcnbritain.org.uk/groups/st-albans

Feel-Good Sunday Afternoons

Third Sunday in the month, 3–5pm

Modern life seems to be increasingly demanding, so once a month we open our hall to provide a small oasis of calm and peace for anyone who wishes to enjoy a short respite from it.

Activities vary according to the season. So far, these have included board games in the spring, a summer afternoon cream tea with entertainment, musical films from the 1950s during the cooler months, and a 'Carols, Cupcakes and Cracker Jokes' afternoon in the run-up to Christmas.

Everyone is welcome, as are ideas for activities that people would enjoy. There is no charge, but donations to the church's charity of the month are always appreciated.

Planning your Visit

You're welcome here!

Here is some information for those planning a visit at either the Homewood or Greenwood site, so that you know what to expect on a Sunday morning.

Where and When?

We meet in the Church for our Sunday Service starting at 10:30am. There is a car park on both sites and street parking is nearby. On arrival you will be greeted by an Elder or Welcomer. Coffee, tea or a soft drink and a biscuit are served from 10am. This is a chance to meet others and find your bearings. All refreshments are free.

Accessibility

There is wheelchair access, and aisles are wide enough to accommodate a walking frame. We have a sound loop for anyone who needs it. There are also disabled toilets.

Our Service

The main service begins at 10:30am with a warm welcome from our Church Secretary or an Elder, and church notices. Then follows a time of songs and hymns, prayers, Bible readings and a sermon. After a Blessing, the congregation is invited to share Joys and Concerns. At Homewood, refreshments are served again, and for those who wish to stay there is a discussion about thoughts raised during the service.

Communion is celebrated once a month at each site. This takes place on the first Sunday of each month at Homewood. All who wish to, are invited to share in the Lord's Supper. The bread is gluten and dairy free and the wine is non-alcoholic.

The services are led by our minister, a visiting minister, a lay preacher or the Worship Group.

Morning services from Homewood are streamed live on YouTube if you'd like to see what happens. You can see these if you click on the link at the bottom of the website page.

Children and Families

At Homewood there is a Children's Corner where books, art and craft activities are provided. Parade Services for the Uniformed Organisations with a connection to the church happen twice a year. These services are led by visiting Christian Youth Workers. Other family activities take place at other times of the week. Please refer to the Children and Families section of the website.

Get in Touch and Stay in Touch

If you would like to let us know about your planned visit, or if you have a question, please email the Church Secretary: secretary@stalbansurc.org

We would be happy for you to take a 'Welcome Leaflet', and if you would like to receive our weekly newsletter by email, or keep in touch with us, please complete a 'Welcome Card' and hand it to an Elder or Welcomer.
