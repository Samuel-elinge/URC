Welcome

St Albans URC is a church that normally meets on two sites – Homewood and Greenwood. Services are held at 10.30am on Sundays at both Homewood and Greenwood – all are welcome.

We recently welcomed our new Minister Revd Naomi Young-Rodas and we look forward to all the new ideas and insights that she will bring to this congregation. She also has responsibility for Newland URC, which is also a church on two sites – Luton and Dunstable.

Our values are firmly rooted in Jesus' call to love God first and then love our neighbours as ourselves. Our hope is that all we do flows from this primary principle. Kindness and a friendly welcome are very important to us.

We seek to be authentic in our encounter with the living Lord. The Holy Spirit is our helper and guide and we try to be open to God's leading; this is a learning process. We hold a wide range of views and hope there is always room for people seeking after truth no matter what their starting point may be.

We are pleased to have been given a Bronze Award in the Eco Church scheme; we are now working for the Silver!

We are part of the Inclusive Church Network. We are a church which celebrates and affirms every person and does not discriminate.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Planning your Visit

You're welcome here!

Here is some information for those planning a visit at either the Homewood or Greenwood site, so that you know what to expect on a Sunday morning.

Where and When?

We meet in the Church for our Sunday Service starting at 10:30am. There is a car park on both sites and street parking is nearby. On arrival you will be greeted by an Elder or Welcomer. Coffee, tea or a soft drink and a biscuit are served from 10am. This is a chance to meet others and find your bearings. All refreshments are free.

Accessibility

There is wheelchair access, and aisles are wide enough to accommodate a walking frame. We have a sound loop for anyone who needs it. There are also disabled toilets.

Our Service

The main service begins at 10:30am with a warm welcome from our Church Secretary or an Elder, and church notices. Then follows a time of songs and hymns, prayers, Bible readings and a sermon. After a Blessing, the congregation is invited to share Joys and Concerns. At Homewood, refreshments are served again, and for those who wish to stay there is a discussion about thoughts raised during the service.

Communion is celebrated once a month at each site. This takes place on the first Sunday of each month at Homewood. All who wish to, are invited to share in the Lord's Supper. The bread is gluten and dairy free and the wine is non-alcoholic.

The services are led by our minister, a visiting minister, a lay preacher or the Worship Group.

Morning services from Homewood are streamed live on YouTube if you'd like to see what happens. You can see these if you click on the link at the bottom of the website page.

Children and Families

At Homewood there is a Children's Corner where books, art and craft activities are provided. Parade Services for the Uniformed Organisations with a connection to the church happen twice a year. These services are led by visiting Christian Youth Workers. Other family activities take place at other times of the week. Please refer to the Children and Families section of the website.

Get in Touch and Stay in Touch

If you would like to let us know about your planned visit, or if you have a question, please email the Church Secretary: secretary@stalbansurc.org

We would be happy for you to take a 'Welcome Leaflet', and if you would like to receive our weekly newsletter by email, or keep in touch with us, please complete a 'Welcome Card' and hand it to an Elder or Welcomer.
