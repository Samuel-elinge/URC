Faith in Action

Trinity Community Project

Trinity Community Project is run by St Albans Mencap and offers day services and activities for adults experiencing mental health issues and/or living with a learning disability. We have regular groups and ad hoc activities throughout the week. We aim to encourage people to participate in a range of social activities, within the service and out in the community, which offer choice and promote people's skills, independence and self-confidence while maintaining their dignity and safety.

Further information: stalbansmencap.org.uk/trinity-community-project or contact our manager at deandavis@stalbansmencap.org.uk

Commitment for Life

Commitment for Life is the URC's global justice programme, run in partnership with Christian Aid and Global Justice Now. Their goal is to support local congregations in making global justice a core foundation of their mission. The programme currently supports projects in Bangladesh, Israel and the Occupied Palestinian Territories, and Zimbabwe. They offer resources to facilitate prayer, advocacy, and effective action for the most vulnerable people in the world.

To find out more: urc.org.uk — Commitment for Life

Food Bank Support

Our church supports local food banks with regular collections of food and other household goods. There is a collection box in the Church Foyer for members, friends and visitors to make donations which are regularly taken to the Verso Care Centre. A special collection is made at Harvest time.

To find out more: verso.church/verso-care/feed

Israel-Palestine

At its General Assembly in 2021 the United Reformed Church (URC) adopted a series of Resolutions on Israel-Palestine. The Resolutions came forward as a result of a visit to Israel-Palestine made by representatives of all 13 Synods of the URC in 2019. The representative for Thames North Synod was a member of Trinity St Albans URC. Prior to that, the church had over many years followed events in Israel-Palestine through Christian Aid and Commitment for Life, and several members had visited Israel and the Occupied West Bank.

Palestinian Christians issued a “Kairos Call” to the world in 2009, appealing for help in the situation they were facing. A gathering of British Christians on Iona laid the basis for a British response, “Time for Action”, and the formation of Kairos Britain (now Sabeel-Kairos UK). Sabeel-Kairos is a UK Christian charity committed to supporting peace and justice in the Holy Land.

Since 2014 members of the URC in St Albans have actively supported Kairos, hosting many events designed to raise awareness of the situation in Israel-Palestine. These have included talks from Zaytoun Olive farmers and from the embroidery cooperative Women in Hebron, as well as reports from local people who have visited the area. There have also been three very popular Palestinian Cultural Evenings featuring costume, music, food and dance.

Members of the church continue to follow the situation through further reports from Palestinian Christians, including the weekly Sabeel Wave of Prayer which relays news and events from the area to be prayed over. Embrace the Middle East is also a source of news and opportunities to support Palestinians.

Planning your Visit

You're welcome here!

Here is some information for those planning a visit at either the Homewood or Greenwood site, so that you know what to expect on a Sunday morning.

Where and When?

We meet in the Church for our Sunday Service starting at 10:30am. There is a car park on both sites and street parking is nearby. On arrival you will be greeted by an Elder or Welcomer. Coffee, tea or a soft drink and a biscuit are served from 10am. This is a chance to meet others and find your bearings. All refreshments are free.

Accessibility

There is wheelchair access, and aisles are wide enough to accommodate a walking frame. We have a sound loop for anyone who needs it. There are also disabled toilets.

Our Service

The main service begins at 10:30am with a warm welcome from our Church Secretary or an Elder, and church notices. Then follows a time of songs and hymns, prayers, Bible readings and a sermon. After a Blessing, the congregation is invited to share Joys and Concerns. At Homewood, refreshments are served again, and for those who wish to stay there is a discussion about thoughts raised during the service.

Communion is celebrated once a month at each site. This takes place on the first Sunday of each month at Homewood. All who wish to, are invited to share in the Lord's Supper. The bread is gluten and dairy free and the wine is non-alcoholic.

The services are led by our minister, a visiting minister, a lay preacher or the Worship Group.

Morning services from Homewood are streamed live on YouTube if you'd like to see what happens. You can see these if you click on the link at the bottom of the website page.

Children and Families

At Homewood there is a Children's Corner where books, art and craft activities are provided. Parade Services for the Uniformed Organisations with a connection to the church happen twice a year. These services are led by visiting Christian Youth Workers. Other family activities take place at other times of the week. Please refer to the Children and Families section of the website.

Get in Touch and Stay in Touch

If you would like to let us know about your planned visit, or if you have a question, please email the Church Secretary: secretary@stalbansurc.org

We would be happy for you to take a 'Welcome Leaflet', and if you would like to receive our weekly newsletter by email, or keep in touch with us, please complete a 'Welcome Card' and hand it to an Elder or Welcomer.
