Community Groups – Homewood

These groups regularly use the Homewood Halls.

Adventure Group

We are a group of people who enjoy being out of doors – walking, cycling etc. Adventure means different things to different people and everyone can choose the right level for them.

We have a regular programme of Wednesday evenings and weekend events. The Wednesday evenings are slide shows, talks, quizzes, meals, and walks or cycle rides in the summer. At weekends we have a walk or cycle ride on Sunday and each month there is at least one weekend trip away to explore the countryside. There are no charges for these events other than the costs incurred, i.e. accommodation, food and transport.

More details: stalbansadventuregroup.org.uk or enquiries@stalbansadventuregroup.org.uk

Wednesday Badminton

Wednesday Badminton Club has been playing for over 30 years at Homewood URC on Wednesday evenings throughout the year except for June, July and August. We are approx. 16 members spanning the decades – mostly with some experience of badminton before joining. We enjoy the game plus social chatter. Vacancies arise from time to time and we welcome enquiries from anyone who might be interested in joining us.

Contact Bob on 01727 866619 or robertpadget@btinternet.com for more information.

Irish Dancing Classes

For children age 4+ from beginners to advanced level. The Academy aims to provide a happy and friendly environment in which children will enjoy learning all aspects of Irish dancing and be inspired to achieve.

Classes are on Saturdays and Wednesdays, age 4+ from beginners to advanced. Register by email to get the first lesson free, limited spaces available.

Contact: walshacademy@hotmail.co.uk or call 07803 375348, website irish-dancing.webs.com

Penny Waterman School of Dance (Homewood)

Ballet, Disco/Jazz, Street Jazz and Musical Theatre classes for children aged 2–17 in St Albans (and nearby towns & villages), and Ballet for adults.

For more information or to book a trial class, visit pennywaterman.com

Rock Choir

Rock Choir offers an alternative experience to the traditional classical or community choir, pioneering a new approach to vocal training and entertainment. We offer the general public the chance to sing pop, rock and chart songs with no entry audition, no requirement to read music or have any previous singing experience. New members are always very welcome and anyone interested in joining can try a free 90-minute taster session.

Homewood Road sessions during term time: Wednesdays 9.45–11.15am, Thursdays 7.30–9pm and 8–9.30pm.

Abigail Harris – St Albans, Hertford & Watford Rock Choir Leader. Enquiries: 01252 714276, rockchoir.com

Evening Rehearsal Orchestra St Albans

We are a group of about 35 players meeting on Monday evenings, about 36 times a year. The music which we play is mainly from the classical orchestral repertoire. No auditions are required and we do not give public concerts, though we do play through what we have been practicing at the end of each term as though it were a concert.

New members are welcome. Please see stalbans-ero.org.uk for more details.

St Albans Macular Group

We meet the second Tuesday of the month at 2.00pm at St Albans URC – Homewood, and there is parking available. The group is friendly and welcomes everyone with sight impairment/low vision. We provide a guest speaker each meeting, tea and an opportunity to socialise, and occasional trips.

For further information please contact Cheri Hunter on 07505 770210 or cherihunter2323@gmail.com

Herts School of Tai Chi Chuan

At Herts School of Tai Chi Chuan, we teach Tai Chi to cultivate physical health, emotional well-being, and mindful awareness. We encourage people to make it a daily practice for body and mind – relaxing tension and developing energy, strength and flexibility, whilst calming anxiety and developing awareness and control of attention. It is never too late (or too early) to start – the movements are gentle, and the focus is on applying internal principles rather than achieving external goals.

St Albans Symphony Orchestra

St Albans Symphony Orchestra brings together professional and amateur musicians of a high standard from Hertfordshire and beyond, developing into one of the UK's leading non-professional ensembles. We present a full season of large-scale concerts in St Albans and the surrounding region, including major symphonic repertoire, concertos with leading soloists, and an annual New Year concert in St Albans Cathedral.

SASO has occasional vacancies for musicians of a good standard, especially strings. We rehearse on Wednesday evenings, 7.30–10pm. Please contact secretary@saso.org.uk, or visit saso.org.uk

Tuesday Badminton

Our friendly ladies' badminton club (formed at least 50 years ago) meets every Tuesday, in school term time only, from 9am to 11am. We are mostly of an average standard and enjoy our games without taking them too seriously, with plenty of social chat in between. We welcome enquiries from anyone interested in joining us – if you fancy some healthy exercise in good company and have at least wielded a racquet before, do come along for a taster session.

Contact Sue on 07717 833485.

DanceCore

Adult Dance Fitness Classes – Fridays 12–1pm during term time. DanceCore is a workout for the Body, Mind & Soul, helping to increase core strength, mobility and coordination through short, fun, easy-to-follow sequences incorporating different styles of dance to uplifting music. No dance experience necessary.

Contact info@dancecore.co.uk, dancecore.co.uk

Caledonian Dancers (Homewood)

The Caledonian Dancers meet most Friday evenings, 8–10pm, from early September to end of May, for an evening of Scottish Country Dancing to recorded music. It's a sociable and fun activity giving both physical and mental exercise and the opportunity to make new friends – you don't need to come with a partner. We dance in sets of 3 or 4 'couples' per set. No special dress is required, just soft shoes, and no previous experience is needed – you'll be helped by the teacher and other dancers.

Your first session is free, thereafter £4 (currently) per evening. Why not give it a go – just come along or contact our secretary at viviennecdavis@yahoo.co.uk

Planning your Visit

You're welcome here!

Here is some information for those planning a visit at either the Homewood or Greenwood site, so that you know what to expect on a Sunday morning.

Where and When?

We meet in the Church for our Sunday Service starting at 10:30am. There is a car park on both sites and street parking is nearby. On arrival you will be greeted by an Elder or Welcomer. Coffee, tea or a soft drink and a biscuit are served from 10am. This is a chance to meet others and find your bearings. All refreshments are free.

Accessibility

There is wheelchair access, and aisles are wide enough to accommodate a walking frame. We have a sound loop for anyone who needs it. There are also disabled toilets.

Our Service

The main service begins at 10:30am with a warm welcome from our Church Secretary or an Elder, and church notices. Then follows a time of songs and hymns, prayers, Bible readings and a sermon. After a Blessing, the congregation is invited to share Joys and Concerns. At Homewood, refreshments are served again, and for those who wish to stay there is a discussion about thoughts raised during the service.

Communion is celebrated once a month at each site. This takes place on the first Sunday of each month at Homewood. All who wish to, are invited to share in the Lord's Supper. The bread is gluten and dairy free and the wine is non-alcoholic.

The services are led by our minister, a visiting minister, a lay preacher or the Worship Group.

Morning services from Homewood are streamed live on YouTube if you'd like to see what happens. You can see these if you click on the link at the bottom of the website page.

Children and Families

At Homewood there is a Children's Corner where books, art and craft activities are provided. Parade Services for the Uniformed Organisations with a connection to the church happen twice a year. These services are led by visiting Christian Youth Workers. Other family activities take place at other times of the week. Please refer to the Children and Families section of the website.

Get in Touch and Stay in Touch

If you would like to let us know about your planned visit, or if you have a question, please email the Church Secretary: secretary@stalbansurc.org

We would be happy for you to take a 'Welcome Leaflet', and if you would like to receive our weekly newsletter by email, or keep in touch with us, please complete a 'Welcome Card' and hand it to an Elder or Welcomer.
