Community Groups – Greenwood

These groups regularly use the Greenwood Halls.

Dance Unlimited

Dance Unlimited was established in the United Reformed Church, Greenwood in Chiswell Green in 1980. We offer classes in Ballet, Tap, Jazz & Body Conditioning for 3–18 year olds.

For more details please contact Andie Baron-Tait — Email: dance_unlimited@btinternet.com · Mobile: 07894 446685

Thursday Yoga

Yoga Thursdays 19.00–20.00. You are invited to experience the physical, emotional and spiritual benefits of yoga. Just bring your mat and a desire to reduce stress, improve flexibility, build strength, and connect with your inner peace.

Please message on 07833 148220 for further details and to book your place. Spaces are limited to keep the classes small, however there may be drop-in places available. — Clodagh.

Soroptimist International St Albans and District

Soroptimist International St Albans and District women's group is the local branch of the global women's organisation, Soroptimist International. Soroptimists "stand up for women and girls" locally, nationally and internationally. Our projects address issues such as violence against women and girls, women's health, education and climate change. We support local organisations like the SAHWR Refuge, Home-Start and Wilder St Albans.

We have regular face to face meetings on the 2nd Monday of most months at St Albans United Reformed Church Hall Greenwood, 7.15pm for 7.30pm start, ending at 9.30pm. Guests are always welcome.

To find out more: sigbi.org/st-albans · email sistalbans@hotmail.com · or phone Jane Slatter on 07834 345796

South St Albans District Rangers & 1st Chiswell Green Brownies

Rangers is for 14–18 year old young women, meeting the 1st, 3rd and 5th Monday of the month during term time, 7.30–9.00pm.

Brownies is for girls aged 7 to 10, meeting each Tuesday during term time, 6–7.30pm.

Brownies and Rangers are all about having fun, trying new things, developing skills, and making new friends. To register your child, visit girlguiding.org.uk

Meditation on Twin Hearts for Physical, Mental and Emotional Wellbeing

A guided meditation created by Master Choa Kok Sui, founder of Pranic Healing and Arhatic Yoga, designed to release negative emotions and enhance physical, mental and emotional wellbeing. This is an in-person, seated meditation. Donations £10, proceeds go to MCKS Charitable Foundation UK (Reg 1188773).

Join Ranjit for the Twin Hearts Meditation every 4th Monday evening, 7.45–9.15pm, at St Albans United Reformed Church, Watford Road, Chiswell Green, AL2 3HG.

For more details contact Ranjit on 07903 234066 or email ranjit@pranichealing.co.uk

Therapeutic Yoga with Moving into Stillness

Mondays 9.30–10.30am. Feel better in your body by exploring what helps you feel well. These 60-minute therapeutic yoga classes with Denise are designed to help you discover and work with your body's needs, combining yoga, somatics, breathing techniques and meditation. No experience necessary — all you need is a yoga mat and a willingness to explore.

For more information: explore@movingintostillness.co.uk or visit movingintostillness.co.uk

Next Stage Choir

Next Stage Choir is led by professional musical director, Russell Scott, and is formed of around 130 singers across 3 choir locations: Marlow, St Albans and Aylesbury. The choir has recorded three commercial albums, toured Sweden, Austria, and most recently the US, where it was featured on national TV. The choir performs regularly around the UK and welcomes new members, working to a high standard whilst enjoying a mix of music from pop and rock to musicals and film music.

More information: nextstagechoir.com

Planning your Visit

You're welcome here!

Here is some information for those planning a visit at either the Homewood or Greenwood site, so that you know what to expect on a Sunday morning.

Where and When?

We meet in the Church for our Sunday Service starting at 10:30am. There is a car park on both sites and street parking is nearby. On arrival you will be greeted by an Elder or Welcomer. Coffee, tea or a soft drink and a biscuit are served from 10am. This is a chance to meet others and find your bearings. All refreshments are free.

Accessibility

There is wheelchair access, and aisles are wide enough to accommodate a walking frame. We have a sound loop for anyone who needs it. There are also disabled toilets.

Our Service

The main service begins at 10:30am with a warm welcome from our Church Secretary or an Elder, and church notices. Then follows a time of songs and hymns, prayers, Bible readings and a sermon. After a Blessing, the congregation is invited to share Joys and Concerns. At Homewood, refreshments are served again, and for those who wish to stay there is a discussion about thoughts raised during the service.

Communion is celebrated once a month at each site. This takes place on the first Sunday of each month at Homewood. All who wish to, are invited to share in the Lord's Supper. The bread is gluten and dairy free and the wine is non-alcoholic.

The services are led by our minister, a visiting minister, a lay preacher or the Worship Group.

Morning services from Homewood are streamed live on YouTube if you'd like to see what happens. You can see these if you click on the link at the bottom of the website page.

Children and Families

At Homewood there is a Children's Corner where books, art and craft activities are provided. Parade Services for the Uniformed Organisations with a connection to the church happen twice a year. These services are led by visiting Christian Youth Workers. Other family activities take place at other times of the week. Please refer to the Children and Families section of the website.

Get in Touch and Stay in Touch

If you would like to let us know about your planned visit, or if you have a question, please email the Church Secretary: secretary@stalbansurc.org

We would be happy for you to take a 'Welcome Leaflet', and if you would like to receive our weekly newsletter by email, or keep in touch with us, please complete a 'Welcome Card' and hand it to an Elder or Welcomer.
